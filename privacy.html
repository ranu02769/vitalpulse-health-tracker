Privacy Policy & Data Safety Disclosures

Application: VitalPulse: BP & Sugar Tracker Developer Account: Personal Developer Effective Date: September 24, 2026 Version: 1.0.0
Google Play Console Privacy Policy URL https://vitalpulse.app/privacy.html

🔒 100% OFFLINE & ON-DEVICE STORAGE DECLARATION

VitalPulse: BP & Sugar Tracker is built on an offline-first architecture. All health records (blood pressure, pulse, glucose levels, medication schedules, notes, and tags) are stored strictly and exclusively on your local Android device in an encrypted SQLite database. We do NOT operate remote servers, we do NOT collect your personal health data, we do NOT transmit health logs over the internet, and we NEVER sell, broker, or monetize your medical information.

1. Introduction & Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how VitalPulse: BP & Sugar Tracker ("we", "us", "our", or "the App") respects your privacy. This policy applies to all users of our Android mobile application downloaded from the Google Play Store.

We are an independent personal developer committed to clinical data integrity and complete patient sovereignty. Because medical vitals represent highly sensitive information, our application is engineered so that no user registration or account creation is required. You can use the application immediately upon installation without providing your email address, phone number, real name, or cloud credentials.

2. Google Play Data Safety Disclosures

Under the Google Play Developer Program Policies, here is our transparent declaration regarding data collection and sharing:

  • Data Collected: No personal, financial, biometric, or health data is collected or transmitted off your device.
  • Data Shared: Zero (0) data points are shared with third parties, external analytics brokers, or advertising networks.
  • Cloud Syncing: Not applicable. The application functions entirely offline without remote cloud storage.
  • Encryption in Transit: Not applicable, as health data is never transmitted across the network.
  • Encryption at Rest: Application data is stored within your Android OS private sandboxed database directory accessible only by VitalPulse.

3. Types of Information Stored Locally on Your Device

When you log information in VitalPulse, the following data points are created and saved locally on your smartphone:

  • Blood Pressure Measurements: Systolic (mmHg), Diastolic (mmHg), Pulse (bpm), Arm Position (Left/Right), Body Posture (Sitting, Lying, Standing), timestamps, and optional user tags (e.g., caffeine, exercise, stress).
  • Blood Glucose Measurements: Value (mg/dL or mmol/L), Meal Timing Context (Fasting, Before Meal, After Meal, Bedtime, Random), timestamps, and dietary triggers.
  • Prescription Pill Schedules: Medication names, dosages (e.g., 500mg, 1 tablet), food timing instructions, reminder times (hour/minute), and daily adherence streak counters.
  • Clinical & Patient Notes: Any subjective notes you type regarding symptoms, headaches, or physician instructions.
  • Application Preferences: Dark/light mode theme selection, glucose display unit preference, primary health focus, and optional 4-digit security PIN hash.

All of the above information stays strictly in your phone's sandboxed local SQLite database managed by Android Room Architecture Components.

4. Android Permissions & Justifications

VitalPulse requests only the minimum required Android runtime and install-time permissions necessary to execute its clinical logging and exact alarm features. We strictly justify each permission in accordance with Google Play's Permissions and APIs that Access Sensitive Information policy:

Permission Type Clinical Purpose & Justification
android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS Runtime (Android 13+) Required to display timely medication reminder notifications and daily resting vitals check alerts. Users can tap notifications directly to confirm doses ("Mark as Taken"). We never send marketing or promotional notifications.
android.permission.RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED Normal (Install-time) When an Android phone restarts, the operating system clears active AlarmManager timers. This permission allows our local VitalAlarmReceiver to re-register your scheduled medication alarms upon device reboot so you do not miss scheduled medication doses.
android.permission.VIBRATE Normal (Install-time) Provides physical haptic confirmation when entering your 4-digit PIN security lock and rings vibrating tactile feedback during medication alarms.

5. Medical & Non-Diagnostic Disclaimer

IMPORTANT MEDICAL NOTICE:

1. Not a Medical Device: VitalPulse: BP & Sugar Tracker is an informational self-monitoring log. It is NOT certified as a medical diagnostic device by the FDA, EMA, or any national medical regulatory authority.

2. No Sensor Measurement Capability: Smartphones do NOT have hardware capabilities to measure physiological blood pressure or blood glucose via cameras, flashlights, or screen glass. Any Play Store app claiming to measure blood pressure with a camera is technically inaccurate. VitalPulse relies solely on calibrated manual entry of measurements taken with certified upper-arm cuffs and approved glucometers.

3. No Medical Advice: Risk categories (AHA 2017 Blood Pressure stages, ADA glycemic targets, Mean Arterial Pressure, Pulse Pressure, and estimated HbA1c) are calculated using published statistical reference formulas for informational visualization only. They do not constitute clinical diagnosis, personalized medical advice, or treatment recommendations. Always consult a licensed physician or cardiologist for medical decisions.

6. Data Retention & User Deletion Rights

In accordance with Google Play's User Data policy and global privacy standards (GDPR, CCPA):

  • Individual Record Deletion: You can permanently delete any individual blood pressure or blood glucose record at any time within the Analytics screen by tapping the trash icon and confirming. The record is instantly purged from local SQLite storage and all statistical calculations are re-computed immediately.
  • Complete Data Purge: You can delete 100% of stored medical logs, medication alarms, and settings at any time by going to your phone's Settings > Apps > VitalPulse > Storage > Clear Storage / Clear Data, or by uninstalling the application.
  • PDF Report Sharing: When you generate a PDF medical report, it is saved strictly in your device's private cache. It is only shared if you explicitly choose to send it via Android's native share sheet (e.g., via your personal email or messaging app directly to your doctor).

7. Third-Party Services & SDKs

VitalPulse does NOT integrate any third-party analytics SDKs (such as Google Analytics for Firebase, Adjust, AppsFlyer, Mixpanel), advertising SDKs (such as Google AdMob, Unity Ads, AppLovin), or data aggregation brokers. Your health behavior is never monitored, profiled, or commercialized.

8. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR)

VitalPulse is intended for adults and individuals managing personal cardiovascular and glycemic health under physician supervision. We do not knowingly market to or collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). Because no data is collected off-device, no child data can ever be transmitted to or stored by us.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect modifications in application features or regulatory requirements on the Google Play Store. Any updates will be posted directly to this public webpage with a revised "Effective Date" at the top of the page.
